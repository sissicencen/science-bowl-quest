Privacy Policy

Science Bowl Quest · Updated November 12, 2026

Science Bowl Quest is a free educational quiz app designed for children and families. This notice describes the behavior of the current offline-friendly version.

Information you provide

The app does not ask for your name, email address, date of birth, account credentials, or contact information. It has no advertising, in-app purchases, analytics SDKs, or social features.

Learning progress on your device

Your quiz attempts, scores, topic statistics, and recent session dates are stored in your device's browser storage (localStorage). The app itself does not send this learning history to us or to an application server. The data is available only in the browser profile where it was saved and does not synchronize between devices.

You can remove the progress from the app's My Progress → Reset local progress control or clear site data in your browser. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data may also remove progress.

Web hosting and technical data

The website is delivered by a third-party web hosting provider. As with other websites, that provider may process technical connection information, such as an IP address and browser information, to deliver and secure the site under its own privacy practices. This is separate from the app's local quiz history.

Children and third-party links

The app is designed for young learners and does not include user-to-user communication or allow children to upload content. It may include a link to an independent science education website; opening that link is optional and subject to the destination site's privacy practices.

Changes to this notice

If features that change how information is handled are introduced, this notice will be updated before those features are made available.

Contact

For questions about privacy, please contact the publisher using the support information provided in the Microsoft Store listing when the app is published.
